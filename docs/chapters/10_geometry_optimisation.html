Sticking point 01

I cannot tell an energy calculation from an optimisation.

Starting question “Why did the program not improve my geometry?”

A more useful question

Explain the difference between a single-point calculation and geometry optimisation. Describe whether the nuclear coordinates change and what evidence in an output distinguishes the two jobs.

Why this helps: It asks what operation the program was instructed to perform.

Compare with an example answer

A single-point calculation evaluates a chosen electronic model at the supplied geometry. Geometry optimisation changes nuclear coordinates to approach a stationary structure according to an algorithm and convergence criteria. A successful single point says nothing about whether the starting geometry is a minimum.

Course-authored teaching example. Your AI response may differ.

Follow-up to ask

Can I evaluate several methods at the same optimised geometry?

Check your understanding

Does a converged single-point energy imply that the molecular geometry was optimised?

Reveal the check answer

No. The coordinates may never have been varied.

Sticking point 02

The energy stopped changing, but the optimiser continues.

Starting question “Why is a small energy change not enough?”

A more useful question

My optimisation has a small energy change but a gradient criterion is unmet. Explain energy, gradient, and displacement criteria conceptually. Ask for the relevant convergence table before suggesting changes.

Why this helps: It identifies the actual unmet criterion and requests the evidence needed to interpret it.

Compare with an example answer

Near a shallow region, successive energies can differ little while forces or geometry steps remain significant. Optimisation programs therefore check more than energy changes. Read all required criteria and the step history. First establish whether progress is normal, oscillatory, or limited by numerical noise before changing thresholds.

Course-authored teaching example. Your AI response may differ.

Follow-up to ask

What would a persistent large gradient tell us?

Check your understanding

Can an almost unchanged energy coexist with an unmet force criterion?

Reveal the check answer

Yes. They measure different aspects of progress.

Sticking point 03

Different starting geometries reach different structures.

Starting question “Which optimised structure is the answer?”

A more useful question

Two initial conformers converge to different geometries at the same method and basis. Explain local and global minima, propose checks of stationary-point character, and describe what a fair energy comparison requires.

Why this helps: It treats the starting geometry and search problem as part of the scientific question.

Compare with an example answer

An optimiser commonly finds a nearby local stationary structure. Multiple valid minima may exist, and one optimisation does not establish a global minimum. Check convergence and vibrational character, compare compatible energies, and explore additional conformers where relevant. The appropriate ranking may require thermal and environmental contributions.

Course-authored teaching example. Your AI response may differ.

Follow-up to ask

How would conformational sampling change my search strategy?

Check your understanding

Does reaching a local minimum prove there is no lower minimum elsewhere?

Reveal the check answer

No. A local search does not establish the global landscape.
